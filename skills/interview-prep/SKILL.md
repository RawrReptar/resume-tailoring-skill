# Interview Prep Skill

Source: AI on Mac Bible, Chapter 33.5 (Interview Prep System)

## Workflow

### Phase 1: Research Package

Given a role and company, generate:

1. **Company deep dive:**
   - Recent news and product direction
   - Financial position and competitive landscape
   - Company culture and values
   - Team/org structure (if discoverable)

2. **Interviewer research** (if interviewer names provided):
   - Background and career path
   - Interests and likely interview lens
   - Published talks, blog posts, or papers

3. **Role analysis:**
   - Likely questions for this specific role
   - Thoughtful answers mapped to user's experience
   - Technical areas likely to be probed

4. **Questions to ask:**
   - 5 thoughtful questions demonstrating research and genuine interest
   - Avoid questions easily answered by the company website

5. **STAR stories to prepare:**
   - 5-7 situations from user's experience mapped to likely questions
   - Format: Situation, Task, Action, Result
   - Include metrics and specific outcomes

6. **Red flags to watch for:**
   - Signs of dysfunction in the interview process
   - Questions that reveal concerning culture
   - Negotiation leverage indicators

### Phase 2: Mock Interview (Interactive)

If the user wants to practice:

1. Play the interviewer role using researched likely questions
2. Ask follow-up questions based on user's answers
3. After each mock answer, provide feedback:
   - What was strong
   - What could be improved
   - Suggested restructuring
4. Iterate until user is comfortable

### Phase 3: Post-Interview Reflection

After the interview:

1. What did the interviewers emphasize?
2. Where did the user feel strongest/weakest?
3. What questions were unexpected?
4. Draft follow-up/thank-you email
5. Log insights for future interviews

## Output Format

```markdown
# Interview Prep: [Role] at [Company]

## Company Research
[Deep dive findings]

## Likely Questions & Prepared Answers
[5-10 questions with mapped answers]

## STAR Stories Ready
[5-7 prepared stories]

## Questions to Ask
[5 questions]

## Red Flags to Watch
[Concerns to monitor]
```

## Principles

- Research should be specific and current, not generic
- Answers should be honest and drawn from real experience
- Mock interviews should be realistic, not softball
- Post-interview reflection improves over time
- Prep materials should be reusable across similar roles
